Waste categories

Separating waste at source makes collection, recycling, and safe disposal easier.

🍌

Wet / biodegradable

Fruit peels, vegetable scraps, leftover food. Compost it or use the wet-waste bin.

📰

Dry / recyclable

Clean paper, cardboard, glass, metal and accepted plastics. Keep them clean and dry.

🔋

Hazardous / e-waste

Batteries, bulbs, medicines, paint and electronics need special handling.

🩹

Sanitary waste

Diapers, pads and bandages. Wrap, mark red and hand over separately.

    Making waste segregation easier for everyone

    EcoSort helps people identify common household waste and understand responsible disposal. Correct segregation reduces contamination and helps recyclables reach the right facilities.

    Always follow your municipal body’s local rules. Accepted materials and collection systems vary by area.

    🌱 Five simple habits

    • Keep wet and dry waste separate.
    • Rinse and dry recyclable containers.
    • Reuse items before throwing them away.
    • Never put batteries or electronics in regular bins.
    • Compost kitchen waste: a small bin with dry leaves or cocopeat works at home.
